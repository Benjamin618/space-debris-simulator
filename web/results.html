This page turns the current validation batch into a short external-facing reading: what improved, what degraded, and what matters most for a LEO proximity tracking demo.

It is intentionally shorter than the Analysis page. The goal is to land on a few defensible conclusions, not to expose every table.

Executive Read

In this dataset, the tracker behaves as expected: repeated radar updates stabilize the estimate, stronger unmodeled acceleration hurts performance, and both revisit rate and measurement precision materially change the tracking error.

The most useful reading is not "the tracker is good" in absolute terms, but rather "the tracker is good under some sensing and motion conditions, and degrades predictably when those conditions worsen."

What this is

A compact interpretation layer over the same batch consumed by Analysis. It is the place where an external reader should understand the main engineering message without opening tables.

What this is not

It is not a proof of orbital realism or a flight-qualified tracker. It is a controlled sandbox showing how a first-order Kalman tracker reacts to sensing cadence, sensing noise, and bounded second-order truth dynamics.
