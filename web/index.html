Controls
  • Mode Start in Player, switch to Greedy anytime
  • Arrows One press = 20 px/s, twice = 40, three times = 80
  • Opposite direction stops the ego on the next allowed command
  • One piloting action is allowed every 1 second
  • Greedy avoids nearby dangerous debris, then chases the closest collectable
  • Battery drain exists even at rest, with an extra cost that scales with speed squared
  • Mission score = collected points - missed threats + sobriety bonus
  • Radar view is ego-centric and displays noisy sensing plus estimated tracks
  • Classification is simulated from object size and observation quality, separate from tracking
  • Space Pause / resume
  • G Toggle grid
  • L Toggle labels
  • V Toggle vectors
  • T Toggle trails
  • R Restart mission
